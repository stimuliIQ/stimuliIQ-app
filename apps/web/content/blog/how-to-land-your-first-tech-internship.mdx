# How to Land Your First Tech Internship in 2026

Getting your first tech internship is one of the most important career steps for a B.Tech or MCA student in India. The competition is fierce, but with the right preparation, you can stand out.

## 1. Build a Project Portfolio First

Recruiters at product companies care about what you've built, not just your CGPA. Start building projects during your second year:

- A **full-stack web application** with a real database (not just a todo app)
- A **data analysis project** using a public dataset from Kaggle
- An **API integration** with a third-party service

## 2. Contribute to Open Source

Even small contributions to popular repositories signal to employers that you can work with real codebases and understand collaboration workflows.

## 3. Practice DSA — But Don't Over-Rotate

Data Structures and Algorithms are still tested at most product companies. Solve 2–3 problems daily on LeetCode, but don't spend 100% of your prep time on DSA at the expense of system design and projects.

## 4. Network on LinkedIn

Connect with StimuliiQ alumni and ask for referrals. A warm referral dramatically increases your chances of getting an interview.

## 5. Apply Early and Often

Start applying 6 months before your internship start date. The best companies fill slots early. Use company career pages, LinkedIn Jobs, and AngelList.
