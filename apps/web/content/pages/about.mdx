# About StimuliiQ

StimuliiQ was founded in 2021 with a single mission: **help India's engineering students land their first job through project-based, industry-grade training**.

## Our Story

We started because we saw a painful gap. Every year, hundreds of thousands of B.Tech and MCA graduates struggle to find their first job — not because they lack intelligence, but because their college curriculum doesn't match what companies actually need.

Our founders — engineers from Hyderabad, Bangalore, and Pune — experienced this gap firsthand. They built StimuliiQ to solve it.

## What We Do

- **Project-based internship programs**: 8–16 week hands-on programs in software development, data science, cloud, and more.
- **Industry mentors**: Every student is mentored by a working engineer or data scientist.
- **Verifiable certificates**: Every certificate carries a unique ID and QR code, verifiable by any employer via a public URL.
- **Placement support**: Resume reviews, mock interviews, and direct referrals to our hiring partner network.

## Our Numbers

- **15,000+** students trained
- **89%** placement rate within 6 months
- **200+** hiring partners across India
- **4.9/5** average student rating

## Our Mission

To give every Indian student — regardless of their college tier — access to the quality training they need to build a successful tech career.
