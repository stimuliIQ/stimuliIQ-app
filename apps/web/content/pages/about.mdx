# About Stimuli IQ

Stimuli IQ is a healthcare education and training platform with a single mission: **help India's medical, psychology, and allied health science students bridge the gap between academics and real practice**.

## Our Story

We started because we saw a painful gap. Every year, thousands of Indian medical and psychology graduates step into their careers without the practical exposure the field actually demands — not because they lack knowledge, but because academic curriculums don't match what real clinical and healthcare practice requires.

Our mentors lived that gap themselves. We built Stimuli IQ to close it.

## What We Do

- **Structured training & internship tracks**: hands-on programs spanning psychology, clinical practice, and allied healthcare.
- **Healthcare mentors**: every student is mentored by a practicing healthcare professional or psychologist.
- **Verifiable certificates**: every certificate carries a unique ID and QR code, verifiable by any employer or institution via a public URL.
- **Career guidance**: portfolio reviews, counselling sessions, and direct referrals through our growing healthcare network.

## Our Mission

To give every Indian student — regardless of their college tier — access to the training and mentorship they need to build a real career in healthcare.
